Title: New Lower Bounds for Erdős–Szekeres Numbers in Three and Four Dimensions

Abstract: We construct eleven points in general position in four-dimensional space with no eight of them in convex position, and fifteen points in general position in three-dimensional space with no eight of them in convex position. The first set raises the lower bound for the least number of points in four-space that forces eight in convex position from eleven to twelve, so that this number is now known to be twelve or thirteen. The second raises the corresponding lower bound in three-space from fourteen to sixteen. Both sets have small integer coordinates, and each proof is a finite list of identities that place one point of the set strictly inside a simplex spanned by others.
